Ever since - to emphasie something that has happened from that moment to still now 
 3 ways to use it 
 As a conjunction, as a preposition, as a adverb especially at last 
 lets come to conjunction
 To use this as conjunction u need to use a Subject verb a proper sentence and then connect the second sentence to it

 ## example
      Ever this i confessed anu , I have had a great control over my habit

 Here i confessed anu itself a strong sentence 
 
 
 To use it as preposition u need to place it before a noun or a noun phase 
 
 ever since my confession to anu, I really have had a great control over my habit 

 To use it as adverb u need to make that sentence as completed action where u can use the cause and effect scenario 

I made my confession to anu , I have really had great control  over my habit ever since
